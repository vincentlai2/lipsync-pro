## Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how LipSync.pro uses cookies and similar technologies on the site.

## What Is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small file stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies can keep you signed in, remember preferences, or help measure site usage.

## Necessary Cookies

We use necessary cookies to operate the service, including cookies that:

- keep your session active;
- secure authentication;
- remember preferences such as language or theme;
- support payments and credit management.

These cookies are required for normal site operation.

## Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics tools to understand viewed pages, traffic, errors, and performance. This helps us improve SEO pages, the generation flow, and product reliability.

## Payment and Third-Party Cookies

Some providers, such as payment, email, hosting, storage, analytics, or AI processing providers, may use their own cookies or similar technologies when their services are loaded.

## Managing Cookies

You can configure your browser to delete or block certain cookies. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent login, payment, or use of the AI lip sync tools.
