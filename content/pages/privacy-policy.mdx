## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LipSync.pro collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our AI lip sync video tools, account features, payment pages, or contact forms.

## Information We Collect

We may process the following categories of information:

- **Account and contact information**: name, email address, login information, and support messages.
- **Uploaded content**: videos, images, audio files, scripts, and other files required to generate lip sync videos.
- **Generation data**: task status, credits used, generated outputs, technical errors, and creation history.
- **Payment data**: selected plan, payment status, transaction identifiers, and billing details handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
- **Technical data**: IP address, browser, device, server logs, visited pages, and analytics data.

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- provide the LipSync.pro AI lip sync tools and generate requested videos;
- manage accounts, credits, plans, billing, and payment status;
- show creation history and make generated outputs available for download;
- protect the service from abuse, fraud, and unauthorized use;
- improve site quality, speed, stability, and conversion flows;
- respond to support requests.

## Uploaded Video, Image, Audio, and Text Files

Uploaded files are used to complete the generation you request. Files may be stored temporarily or retained in your account history so you can access results, depending on available product features. Do not upload content you do not have the right to use.

## Third-Party Services

LipSync.pro may use third-party providers for hosting, storage, payments, email, analytics, and AI processing. These providers process information only as needed to deliver the service.

## Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, secure the platform, and handle support requests. You may contact us to request deletion of your account or certain data.

## Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to information required for operating the service.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data. You may also contact us with privacy questions or concerns.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The version published on this page is the current version.
